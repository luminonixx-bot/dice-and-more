Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

DICE & more ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit diceandmore.com or use our booking form, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you use this website we may collect:

  • Booking details you submit yourself: your name, phone number, the service you're interested in, a preferred date and any notes you add.
  • Usage data, only if you accept our cookie banner: pages visited, approximate location (country/city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Cookie preference: whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, stored locally in your browser.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to and confirm bookings and enquiries.
  • To contact you by phone or WhatsApp about a booking you made.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it (only if you've accepted analytics cookies).
  • To keep the site secure and prevent spam or abuse.

3. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar local-storage technologies:

  • Strictly necessary — remembers your cookie-consent choice so we don't ask again every visit. These are always on, as the site can't function properly without them.
  • Analytics (optional) — Google Analytics, loaded only after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. You can decline these at any time, and you can clear your choice by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. Booking details are sent directly to our team so we can confirm your appointment; they are not shared with third parties except the service providers that help us run the booking system (for example, a messaging platform used purely to notify our team of a new booking) and only to the extent needed to deliver that notification.

5. Data retention

We keep booking enquiries only as long as reasonably needed to fulfil and follow up on your appointment, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at hello@diceandmore.com or WhatsApp.
